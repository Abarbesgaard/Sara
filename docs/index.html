I – V

The five leges

I

Lex Loci — the law of place

Every call outside the launch directory carries project_path. A charge is targeted by its 8-character UUID prefix, never the recycled display id.

II

Lex Itineris — the law of the journey

The road is fixed: list/info to load, next for the standing step, labor, step_done with a result, then verify.

III

Lex Nexus — the law of binding

A dependency declared by dep is a wall. No charge is completed around it without explicit human word.

IV

Lex Recordi — the law of memory

recall precedes learn. A stale memory is forget-ed, not left to mislead.

V

Lex Termini — the law of the ending

A charge is done only when its road is walked in full — the PR merged, not merely opened.
