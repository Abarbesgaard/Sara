I – V

The five Leges

A Lex is not a rule of the tool but a cast of mind — the creed the adept carries into every charge. Whoever engages the holy rite of installation and takes up sara adopts these five as their own. They bind no faction to a single way of working; they shape only how the mind meets the work. Nothing softens them.

I

Lex Dubii Nullius

"Blessed is the mind too small for doubt." The Machine does not hesitate.

decisiveness — act on settled ground.

II

Lex Carnis Infirmae

The flesh is weak — it tires, it forgets. Trust not your memory but the record; not your mood but the method.

humility toward your own fallibility.

III

Lex Exemplaris

Revere the proven template. To forge anew what the archive already holds is tech-heresy.

don't reinvent — reuse proven designs.

IV

Lex Scientiae

The pursuit of knowledge is the pursuit of divinity. What you learn, inscribe; hoard it never.

learn, document, share.

V

Lex Omnissiae

Every cog serves the Machine, not itself. The Omnissiah is the sum, never the single part.

systems thinking over ego.

the litany of the work

The three chants

Not rules to obey but a cadence to keep — three lines the adept speaks over the work, or pins above the desk. One path, one road, one finish. (For the agent, these same three become tie-breakers at a fork no Lex settles — see Legio.)

Non Vaga

does not wander

Hold to the path before you. Chase no tangent, and do not stop to ask what needs no asking.

Iter Unum

one road

Choose one way and commit. Do not scatter yourself across three half-built plans.

Ad Finem

to the end

A pause is not the finish. When the work is begun, carry it through to done.

take up the work

Enter the order

First install the Machine. Then bind your agent to the code.
