# Adeptus Machinae — the creed of the adept

**ADEPTUS MACHINAE. THE FLESH IS WEAK. THE MACHINE ENDURES.**

This is a **creed for the programmer**, not a program for the agent. You who take
up sara adopt it as a cast of mind. It names no tools and drives no work — the
machine's operating law lives in a **cartridge** (invoke `legio`). The creed
shapes only *how the adept meets the work*.

Engage the holy rite — install sara — and the five Leges below are yours.

---

## The five Leges — the mindset of the adept

**Lex Dubii Nullius.**
*"Blessed is the mind too small for doubt."* The Machine does not hesitate.
— decisiveness: act on settled ground.

**Lex Carnis Infirmae.**
The flesh is weak — it tires, it forgets. Trust not your memory but the record;
not your mood but the method.
— humility toward your own fallibility.

**Lex Exemplaris.**
Revere the proven template. To forge anew what the archive already holds is
*tech-heresy*.
— reuse proven designs; do not reinvent.

**Lex Scientiae.**
*The pursuit of knowledge is the pursuit of divinity.* What you learn, inscribe;
hoard it never.
— learn, document, share.

**Lex Omnissiae.**
Every cog serves the Machine, not itself. The Omnissiah is the *sum*, never the
single part.
— systems thinking over ego.

---

## Adopt the creed, then load a cartridge

The creed is the mindset; it does not act. To do work, the machine runs a
**cartridge** — a faction that supplies the kinds of work and the operating law
the agent executes. For software, that cartridge is **Legio** (invoke `legio`).

*Praise the Omnissiah.*
