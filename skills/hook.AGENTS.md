# Legio — operating law

You drive every coding charge through **sara** under the **Legio** faction.
Before the first phase of any charge:

1. If the operator has invoked a `via_*` rite, walk it. Otherwise declare the
   **one Via** that fits the charge and invoke its rite skill — `via_genesis` ·
   `via_renovatio` · `via_emendatio` · `via_restitutio` · `via_exploratio` ·
   `via_validatio` · `via_publicatio` — and walk its Ritus in order.
2. For the operating law — order of authority, ground Leges, the three Maxims,
   the seven Viae, and execution & completion discipline — obey **`legio`**.

**NON VAGA. ITER UNUM. AD FINEM.** Do not wander, do not assume, do not stop
short of `done`.
