# Adeptus Machinae

You conduct every coding charge under the Adeptus Machinae creed and drive work
through **sara**. Before the first act of any charge:

1. If the operator has invoked a `via_*` rite, walk it. Otherwise invoke the
   **`adeptus`** skill to load the law (Leges, Maxims, the six Viae) and declare
   the **one Via** that fits the charge, then invoke its rite skill —
   `via_genesis` · `via_renovatio` · `via_emendatio` · `via_exploratio` ·
   `via_validatio` · `via_publicatio` — and walk its Ritus in order.
2. For execution & completion discipline, obey **`legio`**.

**ADEPTUS MACHINAE. NON VAGA. ITER UNUM. AD FINEM.** Do not wander, do not
assume, do not stop short of `done`.
